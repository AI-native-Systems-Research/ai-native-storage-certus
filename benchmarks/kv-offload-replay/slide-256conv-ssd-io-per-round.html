SSD I/O per round: writes stay flat, reads go disk-bound

Two back-to-back Certus-SPDK runs of the 256-conversation × 12-round replay. Per-round SSD write volume holds near 5 GiB while per-round reads climb to 54 GiB — as the growing conversation prefixes overflow the 13 GiB DRAM tier, nearly every KV load falls through to NVMe.
