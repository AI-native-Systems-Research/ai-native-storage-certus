## MEASURED cost wall — this is the whole story of this component

| configuration | 1 `register` | 2 `register` | 3 `register` + touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| unwind 4, checks ON | FAIL `unwinding assertion loop 0`, 8 s | — | — |
| unwind 6, checks ON | PASS, **253 s** | — | > 620 s |
| unwind 8, checks ON | > 300 s | — | — |
| unwind 4, checks OFF | PASS, 8 s (paths pruned) | PASS, 8 s | PASS, 8 s |
| unwind 4 + swap stub, checks ON | **PASS, 177 s** | > 330 s | > 300 s |

Isolating probes at unwind 4, checks ON: `Vec<Node>::push` 0.47 s PASS;
`HashMap::insert` x2 = the `unwinding assertion loop 0` wall in 5 s;
`BTreeSet::insert` x2 > 170 s. `HashMap::reserve` up front does NOT move the wall.

The wall is `std::ptr::swap_nonoverlapping_bytes::swap_nonoverlapping_chunks::<8>`,
reached from `HashMap::insert`. Its trip count is `size_of::<T>() / 8` — 6 for the
48-byte `Node` — so it is fixed by the TYPE and cannot be shortened by using less data.
Stubbing `std::ptr::swap_nonoverlapping` with a faithful element-wise model removes it and
buys a full-strength pass at unwind 4 — but only for a ONE-`register` scenario (177 s);
two registers still exceed 330 s, because `BTreeSet::insert` is independently expensive.
The refutations and discriminators therefore carry that stub (best chance of closing with
unwinding checks ON inside the gate's 300 s cap); the base harnesses deliberately do NOT,
so their failure is the fast, classifiable `unwinding assertion loop 0` that matches the
documented defeat KD-MULTIMAP-UNWIND-TIMEOUT and needs no artifact levers.

## The vacuity finding — why every divergent base harness carries a `kani::cover!`

At `--unwind 4 --no-unwinding-checks` CBMC does not merely lose the tail of a long loop:
it PRUNES the paths that need more than 4 iterations. Since the real execution of
`register` needs 6, the surviving paths are not the real ones, and assertions about the
real behaviour can pass with no content. Proof from the first full run: every `verify_*`
passed AND 36 `*__mutant` twins — which must fail — also passed.

So for the 9 divergent obligations, where a vacuous pass would score a REAL DEFECT as
`proved`, the base harness does not simply `assert!(spec)`. It binds the outcome, covers
the violating case, then asserts:

    let accepted = p.touch(stale);
    kani::cover!(accepted, "the spec-forbidden acceptance is reachable");
    assert!(!accepted);

An unsatisfiable `kani::cover!` is a FAILED check, so the harness fails whether the
violation is reached (the assert fails) or the path is pruned (the cover fails). A false
obligation can therefore never be scored `proved` on a pruned path.

## Gate risk to flag (not something this lane can fix)

`scorer_kani.py` runs `refute_<id>` with unwinding checks ON at cap 60 -> 300 s and applies
no lever to it. On this component no refutation scenario closes inside 300 s, so a genuine
refutation returns "not proved", the scorer falls through to the base harness, and the
base's known-defeat branch would award `proved` (bounded-shallow) — to a false obligation,
with the mutant check skipped on that branch. The `kani::cover!` guards above are what stop
that here; the general fix belongs in the gate (let the refutation use the same lever
battery as the base, or cap-escalate it).
