KV Cache DRAM Sweep

A three-tier HBM → DRAM → SSD block-LRU cache under trace-derived think-times. Change the workload and re-run the sweep to see where KV lookups land and what the SSD tier absorbs. Every point is a live run of kv_cache_dynamics.py.

Workload

Absolute, or relative to the kv-cache-dynamics directory. Produce one with extract_turn_distribution.py.
Tiers

SSD accepts inf for unbounded. DRAM is the swept axis.

Context & tokens
Scheduling & model

Where lookups are served

Share of in-window prior-context tokens (prefix included) found in each tier, across all measured turns, versus DRAM size.

What the SSD tier absorbs

SSD's share with the tier on, against the recompute (prefill) share when the same sweep runs with no SSD tier — blocks pushed out of DRAM are then lost and the next turn recomputes them.

Decode-stage reads

Share of per-token decode reads served by each tier. A context larger than HBM spills its tail every step, so the lower tiers are read again on each generated token.
